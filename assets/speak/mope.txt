# MOPE's phrase grammar: MOPE (PATCH / FM-1 EDIT held), a gloomy, sarcastic keyboard that listens to what is
# played and comments on it (firmware/src/sp_mope.c). Original text written for Speak37. tools/gen_vocab.py speaks
# and encodes every word like the banks' (build/gen/sp_vocab.h: MO_W, MO_AT, MO_CLS, MO_C_*).
#
#   [NAME]       a class of fragments, one a line. sp_mope.c builds every line fresh from the pools: sometimes an
#                opener (OPEN), the observation (the class of what it heard, or a general template, TPL; for a
#                control its class CT_*, or CTPL), sometimes an aside (ASIDE), a sigh (SIGH) and a closer by its mood
#                (CLOSE, BORED, KIND, RICH); each pool avoids the fragments it gave within its last lines
#   [=NAME]      a slot's values, one a line, in their order (NOTE: the twelve pitch classes from C; QUAL: the chord
#                qualities; IVL: the intervals by semitones 0..12; NUM: the counts 0..16, then "SO MANY"; THING: what
#                a phrase was, by sp_mope.c's MO_K_*; CTL: the controls by its MO_CT_*)
#   $NOTE $CHORD $IVL $N   (in a line) a slot: the note heard, the chord (its note and quality), the interval,
#                the count; $ADJ an adjective of ADJ, $ASIDE an aside of ASIDE, $THING what was played (the chord,
#                the interval, a scale ...) or the control touched; filled when the line is said
#   punctuation  a full stop or a comma is a short pause (and a decimal point on the display), ? a rising word
# Plain words only: every word is said by espeak-ng and LPC-encoded; a word of at most 11 letters (the screen).
